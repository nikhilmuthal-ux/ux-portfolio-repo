    Reflection

    Learning 01

    Realistic data finds what mockups hide

    Long process names, many attributes and deep subprocesses only showed up with real runs, so every screen was tested against real content.

    Learning 02

    Stability builds trust

    An equal split and fixed card heights mean nothing jumps while operators read. A still screen feels reliable.

    Learning 03

    Next time, test with operators earlier

    The weekly reviews were with the PM, developers and the UX team. I would bring operators in from the first rounds.

    Project principle

    Weight follows need. Keep the screen still.
