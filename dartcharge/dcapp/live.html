Register to receive reminders from Dart Charge after you cross to help ensure you never miss another payment.

Location Permission Required (OPT IN)

To send crossing reminders, this app needs to access your device's location data, even when the app is closed or not in use.

Your location data is used to detect crossings and provide reminders. It is not shared and is only used for this feature.

If you enable Geo Location, you agree the app can use your location data for this feature.

After enabling Geo location below, select Always when prompted to help ensure you receive the reminder.

What is a crossing?

  • To determine how many crossings to buy, a crossing is a single journey over the bridge (Southbound) or through the tunnel (Northbound).
  • A return trip is 2 crossings.
  • Payments for future crossings are valid up to 12 months or till they are used.

Pay For Crossings

Use this option to pay for crossings that you made since yesterday or for any crossings you plan to make in the future.

Do not use this option if you are late in paying for your crossings. Late payments will be used for future crossings - you may receive a fine (Penalty Charge Notice) for any late payment.
